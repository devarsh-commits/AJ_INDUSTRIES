About AJ Industries

India-focused industrial automation and electric power solutions.

AJ Industries is a Pune-based supplier of industrial automation products — VFDs, PLC, HMI, servo systems, crane remote controls, crane panels and renewable energy control systems — with hands-on technical support for every installation.

Company Profile

Founded with a vision to make high-quality industrial automation accessible across Indian manufacturing, AJ Industries operates from Pimpri Chinchwad, Pune and serves industrial customers with a complete range of automation products backed by practical on-site support.

  • Authorised dealer and system integrator for Veichi automation and power products
  • Serving manufacturing, process, crane, packaging, textiles and renewable energy sectors
  • Providing supply, installation, commissioning and after-sales service support
  • Registered under GST: 27ACLFA3318C1ZQ  |  PAN: ACLFA3318C
  • Located at MIDC Road, Pimpri Chinchwad, Pune — serving plants across Maharashtra and beyond

What We Stand For

Core capabilities that drive industrial value.

Our strengths are built around helping plants run better — with the right products, correct selection and field-ready support.

VFD & Drive Systems

Veichi AC Drives, low voltage, medium voltage, multi-motor drives and soft starters for pump, fan, crane and compressor applications across industries.

PLC, HMI & Control

TS600 intelligent PLCs, TM700 series, VS-Q HMI and Flex I/O systems for complete machine logic, operator visualization and process automation.

Servo & Motion Control

AC servo systems, DC servo systems, hydraulic servo and general-purpose servo packages for precision indexing, positioning and robot-axis control.

Crane Automation

EOT crane VFDs, wireless crane remote controls and custom-built crane control panels for safe, reliable and code-compliant lifting operations.

Solar & Renewable Energy

Solar water pump systems, hybrid inverters, on-grid/off-grid inverters, BLDC solar pumps and lithium battery solutions for sustainable energy operations.

After-Sales & Support

Site visits, drive commissioning, parameter configuration, fault diagnosis and repair support — ensuring your plant stays operational with minimal downtime.
